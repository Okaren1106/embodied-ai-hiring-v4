 Meet the new industrial revolution 

 The world's most dynamic humanoid robot, Atlas is transforming enterprise applications. Built on decades of research and real-world experience, Boston Dynamics is delivering the new face of industrial automation.

 Stop restructuring your workspace for your robots and start enabling flexible, predictable, and smart material handling with Atlas. 

 Deploy Seamlessly

 Atlas requires minimal supervision for a range of material handling applications and supports barcode scanning and other workflow integrations.

 Work Intelligently

 Built with the latest advancements in AI, when one Atlas learns a new skill, that task can easily be deployed across your entire Atlas fleet.

 Operate Continuously

 Atlas autonomously navigates to a charging station and swaps out its own battery before getting right back on task. 

 Enterprise Intelligence 

 Orbit is your single source of truth: your tool to connect Atlas to MES, WMS, and other systems of record; to oversee Atlas' work and performance; and to review fleet metrics. 

 Today, customers are supercharging their AI and enterprise data strategy with Orbit, and will be able to integrate Atlas into their existing Orbit platform.

 Your Journey to Atlas 

 From first steps to skilled fleets: Our goal is for every customer to see real value with Atlas, delivering feature-rich, autonomous material handling solutions. Boston Dynamics Atlas has already made impressive strides, backed by rigorous field testing, industry-leading AI, and years of experience deploying mobile robots into customer operations. Now we want to hear how Atlas can help you !

 Our team will be in touch with qualified prospects to discuss how dynamic mobile robots can revolutionize your operations.

 1. Prepare

 3. Train

 Commercial Vision

 Electric Atlas

 A new all electric Atlas robot made its debut, starting our journey to a commercial humanoid product.

 Out of the Lab

 First customer pilot

 Atlas is already taking its first steps into a customer facility with Hyundai for field testing on real-world sequencing tasks.

 Enterprise-Ready

 Product version launches

 Our latest iteration of the Atlas robot is built for enterprise applications, incorporating lessons learned from real-world testing and features to support safety, reliability, and ecosystem integrations.

 Building Skills

 End-to-end Solutions
