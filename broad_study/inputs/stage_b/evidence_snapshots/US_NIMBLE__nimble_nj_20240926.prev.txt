 Nimble Launches Robotic Fulfillment Center in New Jersey

 Date:

 Sep 26, 2024

 share:

 link

 Nimble expands multi-node network across the U.S. to scale robotic fulfillment operations
 ‍
 Nimble recently launched its new state-of-the-art warehouse in New Jersey to scale autonomous fulfillment operations. 
 The new fulfillment center will supplement Nimble’s national 3PL fulfillment network with another node in a prime market. This East Coast facility is servicing brands across apparel, footwear, health & beauty, consumer electronics, and more through robotic fulfillment providing industry best SLAs at industry-low costs per unit. “We’re looking forward to scaling autonomous fulfillment to give ecommerce and omni-channel brands access to economical and sustainable 2-day or less logistics across the country,” says Simon Kalouche, Founder and CEO of Nimble. 
 Reach out to Nimble to learn more. 
 About Nimble 
 Nimble is a next generation robotic 3PL specializing in autonomous e-commerce fulfillment to provide industry best SLAs at industry low costs per unit. Nimble's AI technology has picked, packed and handled millions of items across apparel, footwear, health & beauty, electronics, consumer packaged goods, and more for iconic fortune 500 companies to high-growth brands. Nimble’s network of robotic warehouses and transportation solutions are able to save companies up to 40% in total logistics costs while enabling free 2-day delivery for their customers. Nimble’s board of directors includes AI and robotics pioneers who founded Boston Dynamics, Google X, and Waymo.
