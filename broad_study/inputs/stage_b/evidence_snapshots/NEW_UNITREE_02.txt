Intelligent Patrol System
An intelligent patrol system that interfaces with robots, HD cameras, drones and other smart devices—fusing AI-vision algorithms for equipment defects, machinery status, and personnel behavior to enable centralized video monitoring, intelligent inspections, automated responses, and data analysis—comprehensively boosting inspection quality and efficiency while lightening the load on frontline staff.
Digital Twin Platform
Self-Planned Patrols with AI
Large-Scale Inference & Analytics
Hybrid Recognition Architecture
Fast Deployment Capabilities
A 3D-based flight-path auto-generation and robot plug-and-play solution: through a “Waypoint – Simulation – Deployment” closed-loop workflow, it seamlessly connects complex environment modeling, path planning, and on-site execution—enabling rapid deployment and intelligent upgrade of quadruped inspection robots.
Standard Integration Protocol
Rigorously adheres to State Grid Document No. 57 technical specifications, supporting rapid, standardized interfacing with all types of patrol systems and devices.
Intelligent Assisted Point Configuration
Built-in, high-efficiency point-setting tool uses AI models to automatically recognize equipment components, achieving quick and standardized setup of inspection points.
Dynamic Autonomous Inspection
Automatically identifies inspection tasks and target components, then autonomously controls the patrol device to execute inspection missions—dramatically reducing the workload of manual point configuration.
No-Model Meter Recognition
Enhanced meter-recognition capability pinpoints meter locations and types, performs semantic segmentation of measurement ranges, and corrects distortion, automatically reading values without any calibration.
Efficient Implementation Process
Encompasses standardized SOPs for installation, deployment, commissioning, execution, and validation—ensuring high-quality, high-efficiency rollout at every stage.
