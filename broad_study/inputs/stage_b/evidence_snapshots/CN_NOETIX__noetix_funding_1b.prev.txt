 June 22, 2026

 On June 16, China’s National Bureau of Statistics (NBS) released economic data for May 2026. According to the data, the value added of industrial enterprises above designated size increased by 4.5% year-on-year in May, accelerating by 0.4 percentage points from the previous month. From January to May, industrial value added grew by 5.4% year-on-year. Against the backdrop of steady industrial growth, industrial robot output continued to maintain rapid growth.
