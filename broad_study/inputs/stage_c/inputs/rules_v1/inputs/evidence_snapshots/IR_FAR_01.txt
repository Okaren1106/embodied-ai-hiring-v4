Fulfillment Technology and Robotics
Your imagination in action—take your future further
On the Fulfillment Technologies and Robotics team, we build dynamic partnerships between people and intelligent machines. This intricate collaboration helps Amazon fulfill orders with unmatched accuracy. Since we began working with robotics, we’ve added over a million new jobs worldwide.
Working in symphony with our robotic technology, you’ll have the opportunity to extend your technical capabilities by working alongside some of the industry’s most advanced technologies. This includes our fleet of autonomous mobile robots, sophisticated control software, and technologies like language perception, machine learning, object recognition, and semantic understanding of commands. These technologies help us deliver an ever-improving customer and employee experience, as well as improve the safety of our facilities.

Our teams
Amazon Fulfillment Technologies 
Amazon Fulfillment Technologies (AFT) develops tech solutions that create network optimizations for all Amazon sites worldwide.
We own network infrastructure and devices in all buildings and labor planning and scheduling technology for fulfillment centers in North America and Europe.
We are also the authoritative resource for physical inventory in the fulfillment network. 
Amazon Robotics
Amazon Robotics (AR) is at the epicenter of robotics innovation. We design, test, and build mobile robots and mechatronic systems.
Our offices, labs, test floors, and factories are close together, which means we can design the hardware, write the code, test, build, and watch it all come to life.
Our areas of focus include advancements in autonomous mobility, AI, robotic management software, and much more.
Learn more
Frontier AI and Robotics
Frontier AI and Robotics (FAR) is building the next generation of embodied intelligence. FAR drives the development and implementation of advanced AI models within Amazon’s operations that enable robots to see, reason, and act on the world around them, supporting a number of different warehouse automation tasks. Operations will incubate the development of physical AI and physical AI will transform operations.
One Material Handling System
The One Material Handling System (One MHS) team tackles one of Amazon’s most challenging operational issues: lack of standardization across buildings. Recognizing that equipment, controls logic, and operational processes vary significantly from one facility to another due to the use of different Integrators, One MHS aims to create a more standardized approach.
World Wide Design Engineering 
World Wide Design Engineering (WWDE) provides flexible design solutions for Amazon’s global fulfillment network.
We develop conceptual fulfillment design templates for both future and specialty fulfillment buildings and perform firm (detailed) engineering of the equipment, tools, and systems within.
WWDE optimizes the entire delivery value chain to offer comprehensive fulfillment solutions from first to last mile.
