 AGIBOT Humanoid Robots Recognized by IFA Innovation Awards and Global Tech Media at IFA 2026 
 发布时间：2026-09-08 18:46:21 -->

 【 BERLIN, September 8, 2026 】 AGIBOT, a pioneer in the global general-purpose AI robotics industry, earned broad recognition at IFA 2026, with its humanoid robots AGIBOT A3 and AGIBOT X2 honored by the IFA Innovation Awards and leading international technology media. The recognition highlighted AGIBOT’s advances in humanoid mobility, intelligent interaction and multi-robot coordination, as well as the potential of its technologies for real-world applications. 

 AGIBOT receives multiple awards at IFA 2026 

 AGIBOT A3 Earns Broad Recognition 
 The AGIBOT A3 was named a winner in the Best in IFA Next & Emerging Technologies category at the IFA Innovation Awards 2026 . TechRadar named the A3 among its "Best in Show" winners at IFA 2026, highlighting its lightweight, highly dynamic design, touch-based interaction and large-scale swarm coordination capabilities . Techlicious selected the A3 for an “IFA Editor’s Choice” award, citing its combination of dynamic performance, extended endurance and operational readiness for real-world applications. 

 International technology publication BGR further named the AGIBOT A3 one of “The 5 Best Humanoid Robots of IFA 2026,” highlighting its calligraphy capabilities enabled by AGIBOT’s OmniHand dexterous hand, fluid movement and interactive capabilities. 

 At IFA 2026, AGIBOT showcased the A3 through a range of dynamic and interactive demonstrations. Standing 173 cm tall and weighing 55 kg, the full-size humanoid combines dynamic mobility, extended endurance and UWB-based swarm control, supporting coordinated deployment of more than 100 robots. 

 AGIBOT X2 Recognized for Intelligent Service 
 The AGIBOT X2 also received recognition at IFA 2026, earning an Honoree distinction in Best in IFA Next & Emerging Technologies at the IFA Innovation Awards 2026. Gadget Flow selected the X2 among its “Best Products of IFA 2026,” recognizing the compact humanoid robot’s combination of intelligent navigation, human-robot interaction and flexible deployment. 

 Designed for autonomous service in commercial, exhibition and educational environments, the AGIBOT X2 combines autonomous navigation with "Greet–Narrate–Answer" capabilities supporting proactive greeting, multilingual narration, knowledge-based Q&A and expressive interaction. UWB-based fleet control enables coordinated multi-robot operation, while compatibility with LinkCraft and LinkSoul supports customization of motions, expressions and interaction content. 

 The recognition at IFA 2026 reflects AGIBOT’s continued advancement of embodied AI technologies and its efforts to bring humanoid robots into real-world applications at scale.

 AGIBOT is a pioneer in the global general-purpose AI robotics industry, with its core technology focused on embodied AI.
