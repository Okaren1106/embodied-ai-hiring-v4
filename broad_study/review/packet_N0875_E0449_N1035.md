Senior Talent & People Operations Partner
Location: Redwood City, CA
Department: People / Human Resources
Reports to: Director, HR Business Partner

About Dexterity
Dexterity is the enterprise Physical AI company building intelligent robots for complex industrial work. 
Dexterity's Mech systems combine Foresight world model, robotics, and Physical AI platform to perform demanding logistics workflows, including trailer loading, trailer unloading, airport baggage handling, etc. at the speed, reliability, and safety levels required for high-volume operations. Dexterity's Foresight World Model powers real-time perception, planning, and execution across physical environments with the interpretability required for industrial deployment. Dexterity is headquartered in Redwood City, California.
Dexterity is backed by world-class investors such as Kleiner Perkins, Lightspeed Venture Partners, and Obvious Ventures. We are a diverse and multidisciplinary team with a culture built on passion, trust, and dedication.
About the Role
Dexterity is seeking an experienced Senior Talent & People Operations Partner to join our People team during an important stage of growth.
The primary focus of this role is Talent Acquisition. You will own full-cycle recruiting for priority roles across engineering, product, operations, customer-facing, and corporate functions, partnering closely with hiring managers to identify, attract, assess, and hire exceptional talent.
We're looking for someone who brings more than recruiting expertise. The ideal candidate has worked in a fast-moving environment where they've also gained practical experience across People Operations and the employee lifecycle. As capacity and business needs allow, you'll help support broader People initiatives and day-to-day HR needs.
This is a hands-on role for someone who takes ownership, works independently, and is comfortable moving between recruiting priorities and broader People needs.
What You'll Do
Talent Acquisition – Primary Focus
Own full-cycle recruiting for priority roles from intake through offer.
Partner with hiring managers to define role requirements, develop search strategies, assess talent, and drive hiring decisions.
Proactively source and build pipelines for technical, specialized, and difficult-to-fill roles.
Manage recruiting pipelines, hiring velocity, aging requisitions, offer activity, and other key recruiting metrics.
Deliver a high-quality candidate experience throughout the hiring process.
Help improve recruiting processes, interview practices, and hiring-manager effectiveness.
Partner on workforce planning and upcoming hiring priorities.
Support internal mobility and other talent initiatives as needed.
People Operations & HR Support
Support employee lifecycle processes, including onboarding, transfers, promotions, leaves, and offboarding.
Respond to routine employee and manager questions related to People programs, policies, benefits, and processes.
Support HRIS administration, employee data, reporting, and documentation.
Help identify and improve inefficient or manual People processes.
Assist with employee relations, performance, and manager-support matters in partnership with the Director, HR Business Partner.
Support compensation, performance, talent, and employee-development programs as needed.
Assist with immigration coordination and work-authorization tracking in partnership with employees and outside counsel.
Partner on other People initiatives based on business priorities and team capacity.
What We're Looking For
Required
5+ years of progressive recruiting and/or Human Resources experience, with significant hands-on full-cycle recruiting experience.
Demonstrated success recruiting technical, engineering, product, operations, or other specialized talent.
Strong proactive sourcing and pipeline-building capabilities.
Experience partnering directly with hiring managers throughout the recruiting lifecycle.
Practical experience with People Operations and employee lifecycle processes.
Strong communication, organization, and stakeholder-management skills.
Sound judgment and ability to appropriately handle sensitive and confidential information.
Ability to independently manage multiple priorities in a fast-moving environment.
Comfortable operating in an environment where processes and priorities continue to evolve.
Strongly Preferred
Experience in a startup, scale-up, technology, robotics, engineering, manufacturing, or similarly complex organization.
Experience with HRIS and ATS platforms such as ADP Workforce Now, UKG, Lever, or comparable systems.
Exposure to employee relations, performance management, compensation, or talent programs.
Experience coordinating employment-based immigration.
Experience improving recruiting or People Operations processes and systems.
How You'll Work
We're looking for someone who:
Takes ownership and follows through.
Can independently manage searches without requiring significant day-to-day direction.
Builds strong relationships with hiring managers and candidates.
Is comfortable switching between recruiting and People Operations priorities.
Identifies problems and brings forward practical solutions.
Can create structure without adding unnecessary process.
Thrives in an evolving, fast-moving environment.
What Success Looks Like
Within your first year, you will have:
Taken ownership of priority recruiting and helped improve hiring velocity.
Built proactive pipelines for critical and difficult-to-fill positions.
Established strong partnerships with hiring managers.
Improved the consistency and candidate experience of our recruiting process.
Become a reliable resource for broader People Operations needs.
Taken ownership of recurring People processes and projects that create additional capacity across the team.
Compensation
Base pay is one component of Dexterity's Total Rewards package, which may also include equity, comprehensive medical, dental, and vision benefits, and other incentives depending on eligibility.
The annual base salary range for this position is $135,000–$170,000, depending on experience, skills, scope, and overall qualifications.
Final compensation will be determined based on relevant experience, qualifications, market factors, and internal equity considerations.

JOB SUMMARY
We are seeking a highly experienced, versatile, and execution-oriented Technical Sourcer to lead sourcing strategy and execution for our fast-paced hiring needs across software, hardware, and product teams.
In this role, you will not be restricted to a single technical specialty; instead, you will apply deep sourcing expertise across multiple disciplines, shaping sourcing standards and pipeline strategy for our Mountain View, CA office. Additionally, as the senior recruiting team member and sole recruiting presence in our Mountain View location, you will serve as the trusted advisor and operational leader for local hiring—partnering with local site leadership and cross-functional interviewing teams to design, coordinate, and continuously improve all CA office-specific on-site interview loops.
ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES or KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES
Partner with recruiters, hiring managers, and site leadership to shape sourcing strategy across diverse engineering domains, including Hardware (ME, EE, Embedded), Software, and Product roles, translating ambiguous hiring needs into scalable sourcing plans.
Lead the sourcing of qualified passive candidates using various channels, including LinkedIn Recruiter, GitHub, technical publications, and active networking within deep tech, software, and hardware communities, continually refining channel strategy and sharing best practices that raise sourcing effectiveness across the team.
Screen resumes and conduct initial vetting calls, exercising sound judgment to assess technical baseline capability, alignment with role scope, and cultural add, while coaching others on effective screening practices.
Serve as the primary on-site Talent Acquisition representative and trusted advisor for the Mountain View office. Partner with local site leadership and cross-functional interviewing teams to design, coordinate, host, and continuously improve all CA-specific on-site interview loops, ensuring a seamless, high-touch experience.
Own candidate pipeline management and applicant tracking system (Greenhouse) data integrity, identifying and resolving process gaps that affect data quality or compliance.
Analyze pipeline and sourcing data to evaluate program effectiveness, delivering data-driven insights and recommendations to hiring managers and the broader People team on talent market conditions across software, hardware, and product landscapes.
SKILLS AND REQUIREMENTS
Deep expertise in technical sourcing, with a proven ability to fluently pivot between software, hardware, and product management domains and apply sound judgment to ambiguous or complex sourcing challenges.
Track record of designing and scaling creative sourcing strategies that unearth niche talent beyond standard job postings, and sharing techniques that raise sourcing standards across the team.
Demonstrated ability to build trusted, influential partnerships with local Mountain View site leadership, diverse interview teams, and candidates alike, shaping how the function engages with the site.
Outstanding organizational skills with a proven ability to independently manage real-time interview logistics, scheduling variables, and physical on-site hosting, while improving processes for scalability.
Comfortable operating as the senior recruiting presence in a satellite office; self-motivated, proactive, and resilient through changing business and engineering priorities, exercising independent judgment with minimal oversight.
High proficiency with modern applicant tracking systems (e.g., Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) and sourcing discovery tools, with the ability to identify and address data quality or reporting gaps.
Embodies the company values of Curiosity, Humility, Integrity, Passion, and Creativity (CHIPC).
EDUCATION and/or EXPERIENCE
8+ years of technical sourcing or full-cycle recruiting experience in a fast-paced startup or high-growth technology environment, with demonstrated impact at a senior or staff level.
Demonstrated track record of leading talent-mapping and pipeline-building strategy across a mixed portfolio of hardware, software, and product roles, and influencing sourcing standards beyond a single team.
Prior experience serving as a trusted advisor to local office stakeholders and independently leading end-to-end interview coordination loops.
