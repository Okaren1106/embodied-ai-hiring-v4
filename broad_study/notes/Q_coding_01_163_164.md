L1 About the Job
L2 Field AI is building the future of autonomy—from rugged terrain to real-world deployment. We’re on a mission to develop intelligent, adaptable robotic systems that operate beyond simulation and thrive in unpredictable environments. As our Robotics Autonomy Engineer – Triage, you’ll be the fastest link between what our robots experience in the field and how our autonomy stack improves, reproducing behaviors, finding root causes, and driving rapid fixes and tests that make every deployment smoother than the last. You’ll be part of a deeply technical team advancing real-world robotic capabilities through cutting-edge research, simulation tools, and field validation. If solving hard puzzles on real robots and seeing your fixes land in the field within days excites you, and you want to work where your code hits the ground (literally)—this is your role. This is Field AI
L3 What You’ll Get To Do
L4 Triage incoming reports from field deployments and customer sites, assess impact, and decide what gets fixed now, what gets routed, and what gets tracked.
L5 Reproduce field behaviors from logs, telemetry, and recordings, both in simulation and on hardware.
L6 Root cause behaviors that span perception, localization, planning, control, and integration boundaries, and ship targeted fixes quickly.
L7 Turn every fix into a regression test, scenario, or hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) check so the improvement holds.
L8 Build triage tooling for log analysis, event detection, and trend tracking across robots and sites.
L9 Run structured test campaigns before releases and help decide when a build is ready for the field.
L10 Share clear findings and summaries with owning teams, and champion design improvements when patterns emerge.
L11 Travel occasionally to deployment sites to see robots operate firsthand and support field teams

L3 As an Assembly Technician for Perception & Compute Payloads, you will be a hands-on contributor responsible for assembling, integrating, and validating Field AI’s sensing and compute payloads. You will support both prototype development and production workflows while working closely with engineers to improve build processes and increase system reliability.

L15 3. System Testing & Quality Assurance

L17 Document test results, deviations, rework, and repair actions with high accuracy.

L19 Conduct inspections for workmanship quality, mechanical stability, and electrical safety.
L20 4. Debugging & Continuous Improvement
