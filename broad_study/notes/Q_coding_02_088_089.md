
## 88 E0374 US_APPTRONIK Electrical Engineer
[full_description]
L1 Apptronik is a human-centered robotics company developing AI-powered robots to support humanity in every facet of life. Our flagship humanoid robot, Apollo, is built to collaborate thoughtfully with people, starting with critical industries such as manufacturing and logistics, with future applications in healthcare, the home, and beyond.
L2 We operate at the cutting edge of Applied AI, applying our expertise across the full robotics stack to solve some of society's most important problems. You will join a team dedicated to bringing Apollo to market at scale, tackling the complex challenges like safety, commercialization, and mass production to change the world for the better.
L3 JOB SUMMARY
L4 We're looking for Electrical Engineers to develop electronics for the Apollo humanoid robot who embody our company values of creativity, humility, integrity, passion, and curiosity. Robotics is a multidisciplinary domain, and we highly value strong technical first-principle thinkers with strong electrical engineering fundamentals in this role.
L5 ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES or KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES
L6 Design, simulate, implement, and test multi-layer mixed signal PCBAs.
L7 Rapid prototyping of electrical systems at both system and board levels.
L8 Simulate and verify electronics and electrical system requirements.
L9 Generate design documentation such as schematics, block diagrams, test plans and procedures, fabrication and assembly drawings.
L10 Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, including software, mechanical, thermal, systems engineers, and manufacturing.
L11 Execute comprehensive validation and test plans, design necessary test fixtures, and execute rigorous testing to confirm compliance with performance specifications and regulatory standards (e.g., FCC, CE, UL).
L12 Participate in the debugging and root cause analysis related to hardware and software defect resolution.
L13 Provide ongoing support of existing designs.
L14 SKILLS AND REQUIREMENTS
L15 Familiarity with CAD tools including Altium or similar tools.
L16 Proven experience designing, prototyping, and debugging PCB/PCBAs.
L17 Familiarity/experience with electronics lab tools and simulation tools (ex: LTSpice, PSpice).
L18 Excellent communication (verbal & written); will be expected to develop detailed build assembly instructions and diagrams.
L19 Excellent problem-solving and hardware debugging skills.
L20 Hands-on experience with prototyping and testing, including the use of lab equipment.
L21 Travel up to 10% within the US and internationally.
L22 EDUCATION and/or EXPERIENCE
L23 Bachelors, Masters, or PhD degree (or equivalent) in Electrical Engineering or related field.
L24 2+ years of experience in electrical engineering.
L25 NICE TO HAVE:
L26 Prior experience designing electronics for lightweight, space-constrained systems
L27 Experience implementing embedded systems on robots and troubleshooting hardware
L28 Knowledge of emerging technologies such as AI accelerators or quantum computing is a plus
L29 PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS
L30 Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
L31 Must be able to lift 15 pounds at times
L32 Vision to read printed materials and a computer screen
