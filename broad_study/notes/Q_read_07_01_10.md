● Conduct advanced preventive and corrective maintenance inspections to proactively identify and resolve highly complex facility and equipment issues.
● Diagnose, troubleshoot, and repair complex problems in plumbing, electrical, HVAC, and mechanical systems independently.
● Perform advanced skilled trades work including carpentry, painting, infrastructure upgrades, and specialized installations.
● Install, program, and calibrate sophisticated building systems and equipment in compliance with codes, regulations, and manufacturer specifications.
● Lead or coordinate complex maintenance and construction projects, ensuring technical accuracy and quality outcomes.
● Respond to escalated work orders and service requests, prioritizing tasks to maintain operational continuity.
● Maintain comprehensive records of maintenance activities, materials usage, and compliance documentation.
● Manage parts, tools, and supply inventories to support advanced maintenance work.
● Provide technical training, guidance, and mentoring to maintenance staff across levels.
● Act as a resource for cross-functional teams and contractors on facilities maintenance best practices.

● Highly developed problem-solving and analytical skills to address complex, non-routine issues independently.
