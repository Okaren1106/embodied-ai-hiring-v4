L2 This role is equal parts tactical buying, prototype planning, and cross-functional firefighting. L3 Key Responsibilities
L4 NPI Material Planning
L5 • Convert engineering build plans and prototype schedules into time-phased material requirements for EVT/DVT/PVT and pilot builds.
L6 • Plan against incomplete and rapidly changing BOMs; run BOM scrubs with Engineering to close gaps on part numbers, revisions, quantities, and reference designators before they become shortages.
L7 • Identify long-lead and single-source parts early and drive risk-buy or bridge-buy decisions with Global Supply Managers.
L8 • Build and maintain the NPI shortage list; publish clear-to-build status and recovery dates for each build event.

L20 • Maintain inventory accuracy for NPI stock at quick turn suppliers, including reconciliation of Figure-consigned material.
L21 Transition to Production
