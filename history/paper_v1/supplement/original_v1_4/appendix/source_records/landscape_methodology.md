# Living landscape methodology

## Scope and unit of analysis

The unit is a legally or operationally identifiable company with public evidence of meaningful activity in humanoid robots, general-purpose embodied AI, robot foundation models, robot learning, manipulation, mobile manipulation, deployment, or humanoid manufacturing. A large technology or automotive group enters only when its embodied-AI operation or relevant jobs can be separated from unrelated activity.

## Provisional inclusion rule

- `CORE`: direct official evidence of an integrated humanoid/general-purpose robot, a robot foundation-model program, or an embodied-AI platform plus ongoing product, deployment, or hiring activity.
- `ADJACENT`: specialized hardware/software or a large-company team that materially supplies the embodied-AI labor market but is not a standalone full-stack company.
- `UNCERTAIN`: credible signal exists, but identity, continuity, product scope, or current activity is not sufficiently verified.
- `EXCLUDE`: traditional automation, industrial robotics, or AI activity without a distinct embodied-AI connection under this project's scope.

Candidates are retained with their code and reason; uncertainty is not silently deleted.

## Evidence and status coding

Status dimensions are independent: `research`, `prototype`, `external_pilot`, `commercial_delivery`, `deployment`, `manufacturing_scale_up`, and `scaled_commercial_operation`. Each uses `YES`, `NO_EVIDENCE`, or `UNKNOWN`; absence of public evidence is never coded as factual absence. Strong claims require an evidence-ledger row with source date, retrieval date, URL, and confidence.

Evidence types remain separate:

1. **Observed fact** — directly stated or visible in a source.
2. **Derived variable** — generated by a documented rule from observed fields.
3. **Inference** — an interpretation or hypothesis, labeled as such.

Company releases are acceptable evidence of what a company announced, not independent proof of success. Commercial delivery does not imply scaled commercial operation. One requisition is not one vacancy or one hire.

## Hiring-data feasibility rubric

The categorical assessment is an internal sampling aid, not a scientific index.

- `EXCELLENT`: public structured endpoint, stable IDs and URLs, full descriptions and useful metadata, no login, repeat snapshots feasible.
- `GOOD`: public structured board or highly regular pages with stable identifiers and mostly complete descriptions.
- `USABLE`: public jobs and full descriptions, but dates, departments, IDs, pagination, or historical access are incomplete.
- `DIFFICULT`: public access is fragmented, dynamic, mixed with unrelated group jobs, or requires substantial manual filtering.
- `POOR`: no reliable public source found, login/protection blocks access, or postings are too sparse to support a pilot.

Old/closed-job retention is coded only when directly checked. Longitudinal feasibility means repeated public snapshots can plausibly identify additions/removals; it does not validate that removals equal hires.

Search endpoints must be interpreted according to their semantics. Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby board totals are current record counts, while Workday `searchText` behaves as full-text search: overlapping query hits cannot be added or treated as classified role counts. Store both the company's home country and each posting's work location. NVIDIA's sampled humanoid roles in Shanghai show why company nationality is not job geography.

## 4+4 pilot validation and first-snapshot protocol

Company-level pilot approval and source-level collection approval are separate decisions. During
the 2026-08-30 validation phase, public official sources requiring no login were checked for
exactly 5–10 postings. On 2026-08-31, humans approved exactly the eight validated source tuples for
one initial full-board snapshot. Each snapshot preserves raw evidence, source-native identifiers,
canonical URLs, retrieval time, normalization counts, missingness and duplicate candidates.

Login-required or potentially session-dependent sources receive a pending human review record.
The project does not use credentials, continue through login, or bypass CAPTCHA, request signing,
encryption, CSRF protections, rate limits, or other access controls without approval; approval
never authorizes bypass. A failed TLS, HTTP, browser, or runtime attempt is coded
`UNKNOWN_NOT_ZERO_HIRING`. It cannot generate an empty hiring observation.

The bilingual role taxonomy v0.1 is a draft codebook only. Role Family and Work Orientation use a
single primary label when determinable plus optional secondary labels; Technical Domain and Robot
Scope are zero-to-many. The 30 hard-case judgments were initially produced by ChatGPT and then
reviewed by the researcher for operational-codebook development. They are not independent
double-coded gold-standard data and are ineligible for inter-rater agreement. Candidate batch coding is permitted with explicit experimental purpose and versioning. Formal measurement claims require independent validation. Pilot validation therefore supports source
feasibility, not China–US comparison.

### Historical Sim/Eval interpretation (superseded for current candidate coding)

Sim/Eval Structural Review A closed on 2026-09-06 with
`overall_sim_eval_structure=SPLIT_CANDIDATE`. This closes the structural review question only. It
does not create, name, define, activate, or freeze replacement L1 Role Families;
`replacement_family_freeze=PENDING_ADDITIONAL_EVIDENCE` remains in force. The 35-case result is a
researcher-confirmed closeout of an AI-agent review set, not independent double coding, classifier
validation, or a gold standard.

For this project, Role Family represents skill-bundle and job-design structure in the talent
market rather than an HR-title taxonomy. Determine the family from all four of the following:

1. why the headcount exists;
2. the principal work object;
3. the irreplaceable professional capability used continuously; and
4. whether the role would still basically exist if that capability were removed.

Product, Research, Software, and TPM wording may describe work orientation, work mode, or
organizational wrapping; it does not automatically determine Role Family. A candidate technical
family may be primary only when that technical system or capability is itself the owned object.
Simulation or Evaluation used only for training, validation, a quality gate, development tooling,
or a program component is secondary. Evaluation-primary requires evaluation, measurement,
benchmark, or assessment capability itself to be a principal owned output; ordinary QA,
manufacturing quality, and acceptance testing do not qualify. Simulation-primary can cover direct
engineering or explicit ownership of a simulation platform, environment, or product; the role does
not have to write simulator code personally.

Structural Review B uses purposive positive-case supplementation rather than a representative or
high-recall design. It may inspect the frozen baseline and clearly separated public, official,
no-login external postings to identify recurring owned objects and indispensable skill bundles.
External supplements never enter the 610-row baseline or prevalence denominators. Previously
exposed cases and Review A calibration anchors retain explicit provenance and cannot be presented
as independent blind evidence. Boundary cases are deliberately retained to test whether
Simulation/Evaluation is primary, secondary, or incidental. A positive-target batch may support
provisional definition drafting, but it cannot validate coverage, estimate frequency, establish
coder accuracy, or authorize a taxonomy freeze.

Validation-readiness is label-specific and does not imply validation or taxonomy approval.
Work Orientation primary and secondary assignment statuses are recorded separately from ontology
dependencies and provisional candidates. `WO_PRODUCTION_SCALE` is disabled for assignment while
its physical-production and broad-scale candidate definitions remain unresolved.

The first snapshot establishes `first_seen` and `NEW_JOB` baseline events only. It cannot establish
closure, reposting or reactivation behavior. Whole-firm nontechnical jobs remain in the collected
board; analytical inclusion is deferred to a frozen taxonomy and sample rule. Current board
records are requisitions, not hires or headcount. Weekly collection is a post-review default, but
the machine schedule is configured ACTIVE for Sunday 20:00 Asia/Shanghai; actual per-source success is recorded in run manifests.

The first formal baseline received sample-based human QA: 64 researcher-reviewed records plus an
independent 16-record check (2 per company). No systematic extraction, parsing, or normalization
error was observed, so extraction QA is `PASS`. This is not a claim that all 610 records were
manually verified. The baseline is frozen as longitudinal t0.

Deduplication is non-destructive and operates first as candidate generation. The raw observation
unit remains the source-native posting/requisition. Similar titles, JDs, requisitions, locations,
or content hashes do not by themselves authorize row deletion. A future analytical
`canonical_job_group_id` may represent an adjudicated underlying hiring-intent hypothesis, but it
must live in a separate versioned mapping with evidence and must not replace source identifiers.
Repost, reactivation, and closure require multiple comparable successful snapshots.

## Research tier rubric

- `A`: strong pilot candidate balancing industry relevance, route/stage diversity, separable embodied-AI jobs, and feasible public data.
- `B`: useful full-sample candidate with high research value but at least one accessibility or comparability limitation.
- `C`: contextual or optional; useful for taxonomy, but likely sparse, adjacent, or hard to compare.
- `D`: exclude from pilot or investigate manually before inclusion.

## Update protocol

1. Read the current CSVs and latest `data/history/<date>` snapshot.
2. Recheck official product/news and hiring URLs; append the retrieval time, method, HTTP result, protection and observed artifact to `data/source_access_log.csv`.
3. Append new evidence; never overwrite a claim without a replacement note.
4. Change current company fields only when the new evidence supports the change.
5. Run `python scripts/landscape_update.py --date YYYY-MM-DD` to validate IDs, create a snapshot and emit a row-level delta.
6. Manually interpret deltas against the evidence ledger before changing research design.

## Known measurement limitations

Public pages are selected disclosures; ATS fields and job-board scope differ across firms and countries. Job counts can change because of page design, duplicate locations, requisition reuse, or collection failure. Access failure is also environment-specific: an SSL error, 403, browser connection close or security redirect is not evidence that a source or company has no jobs. China–US contrasts therefore describe this first-round public-evidence sample, not national populations or technological leadership.
