# Supplementary Material

Technology Mentions and Direct Qualification Expressions in Embodied AI Recruitment

## Corpus construction and provenance

The initial wave required readable public job details, stable identifiers and core fields; it yielded eight firms and 610 advertisements. The expansion considered the remaining entities in the existing company landscape using organizational identifiability, research relevance, source quality and a current official board with a defensible boundary. Failed sources were recorded before the next feasible candidate was considered. The resulting 1,654 advertisements are a purposive snapshot, and neither wave provides an exhaustive ranking of excluded firms.

The 23-company comparison excludes the 24-record Amazon Frontier AI and Robotics team because it is bounded within Amazon. The 84-advertisement work-object case set covers machine-learning and artificial-intelligence (ML/AI) and software functions at four earlier case-priority firms; no preserved outcome-blind rule supports generalizing from those four. The 248-advertisement task sample instead applies a recorded function-by-primary-task intersection. Its technology catalog was fixed after an earlier pilot, and previous findings were known when the revision protocols were recorded. All analyses are exploratory.

Composite identity joins corpus, source advertisement ID and text-version hash. It produces exact matches for all task advertisements and avoids a duplicated convenience ID outside the task sample. Headquarters comes from company metadata, location from the captured advertisement or accepted reference, and language from source text. Six department-only location values are retained but excluded from geographic coverage. The language rule uses title plus the fullest body field, leaves strings with fewer than 40 Han-plus-Latin letters unclassified, and requires a unique leading Latin-language marker score. Country and location do not enter this classifier, and no advertisement was translated for coding.

## Functions activities and task membership

Primary function follows the continuing deliverable. Composite records contain several independent deliverables of similar weight; multiple-direction records explicitly recruit into alternative directions. Ambiguous, unmapped and source-insufficient cases remain visible. Activities are coded separately: research requires a concrete problem or object plus methodological exploration or scientific testing; engineering requires implementation, integration, debugging or operational delivery; and quality includes verification, compliance, release decisions or defect closure. Work objects in the four-firm case set may overlap, whereas primary task for the technology analysis is single-valued and follows the role’s main responsibility.

The author reviewed the accepted corpus and settled disputed category designs and central rules, including attention to what the role actually delivers and does. The preserved release supports this project-level account but not a row-level independent second code. Historical model-review depth remains 469 full-text, 760 boundary-review and 347 carried-forward records for specific function, with 56/164/28 in the task sample. These strata do not become human-review counts.

## Qualification rules

Mention requires an explicit source occurrence. GENERAL and REQUIRED use both heading scope and local modifiers. Ordinary headings such as “Requirements”, “Qualifications” or “Skills and Requirements” are general; only an explicit required/must/mandatory scope makes an occurrence $R$. A statement under $R$ is not automatically duplicated into $G$, although distinct statements can make both positive. Choice, example and preference operators propagate over their governed items, and a local operator overrides a broad heading. A separate unconditional occurrence remains positive.

The target is exact. C++ is not inferred merely from C; Linux is not inferred from Unix; Altium does not include unnamed printed-circuit-board (PCB) tools. The electromagnetic-compatibility, signal-integrity and power-integrity (EMC/SI/PI) indicator is the sole prespecified family and unions named compatibility, interference, immunity, signal-integrity and power-integrity methods once per advertisement. $U$ is used for unresolved source scope, not as a generic response to reader disagreement. $Z$ means no positive within adequate captured source and the rule, not employer absence. $Q$ is derived from $G$ and $R$ only after each constituent is resolved.

## Diagnostic execution and adjudication

The diagnostic used seed 20260921 to order records within each task, technology and prior-$G$ stratum. Up to five cases were retained from each of positive, unresolved, nonpositive-with-mention and no-mention strata, producing 105 pairs and 94 advertisements. This deliberately enriches boundary states; no probability weight is assigned to the match counts.

On 21 September 2026, GPT-5.6 Sol read the seven source packets under the saved rule sheet. Composite identity, employer metadata, earlier states, rationales and stratum were absent; target, task, title, original-language text and mechanically redacted source fields were visible. The reader also had prior exposure to a mandatory workspace summary containing aggregate project history. Its 105 judgments were frozen at SHA-256 `8f1ef32800186617347ae3af7e3109e48a0c5ac34ca8b1b5b23ca9e45dd25bbb` before comparison. The rule-sheet hash is `bb5948cdab7131a2614c073a9668b990ec9e9254f41436f4ed895f8f706784d2`; the consolidated packet hash is `83d8787cde2c092e20ba9464cb7c6dc3d97516f9231c3ea7aa24a6e6741bad1e`.

GPT-6 Astra, in the coordinating task, then reread all 16 $Q$ differences and six additional $G/R$ differences. The revision hierarchy is evidence based: three explicit-example PyTorch cases enter the current primary layer; ten slash/list cases retain the primary adjudication but admit the sole observed defensible alternative; three scope-inconsistent $Q$ readings and six $Q$-unchanged heading/bucket readings enter only the all-fresh stress test. Five task flags cover four advertisements. Three advertisements flagged only for task support were not independently reread by the coordinating adjudicator; their flags are preserved for a membership restriction. Deployment labels are reported as observed. No backend snapshot, decoding parameter or model-generation seed is available.

The ten contested records form seven switches. C/C++ is coupled across A043/A080; the two targets in the same Python/PyTorch clause are coupled; and two advertisements using the identical EMC “including” template are coupled. Four singleton switches cover a trailing-`等` skills list, Qt/C++, PyTorch/TensorFlow as choice, and Linux/Unix as a joint list. For the last, Linux must be directly named under the chosen slash reading: Unix is never substituted for Linux. Enumerating all $2^7=128$ coherent combinations gives a semantic-reading envelope without inventing a third state or attaching probabilities. This envelope is kept distinct from the $U$-assignment bounds within each combination. The all-fresh scenario substitutes all 105 new $G/R$ outputs and is labelled an extreme stress test because it also imports readings rejected by the fixed rules. Exact quotes, reasons, coupling and hashes appear in the provenance ledger and process table.

## Weighting comparisons and local decomposition

Advertisement-weighted estimates describe observed advertisements; firm-equal estimates average valid firm shares. Eligibility requires minimum evaluable counts separately for $M$, $G$ and $Q$, at thresholds ten and five. We report the corresponding $B$-only coverage so that exclusion due to unknown states is visible. Scenario comparisons use both dynamically eligible pairs and the fixed corrected-primary keys; identity changes cannot be mistaken for value changes.

The Galbot–Figure decomposition uses 82 Galbot Moka advertisements observed on 11 September and 60 Figure Greenhouse advertisements observed on 31 August. All contain captured body text and enter its activity denominator. Galbot sources are Chinese; Figure contains 51 English, four Spanish and five Portuguese advertisements. A symmetric Kitagawa identity decomposes the pooled difference into function composition and within-function rates. These checks concern the 142 local records and do not alter the whole-board source-quality screens, campus/internship exclusion or other previously defined sensitivities.

## Examples of REQUIRED only and clarified expressions

Five advertisement–technology pairs in four advertisements are positive only under an explicitly required scope. The Chinese excerpts below are followed by labelled translations; the two English excerpts are reproduced verbatim.

- **E0206, PyTorch:** under “硬性要求” [mandatory requirements], “深度学习框架熟练(PyTorch 为主);能独立推进实验闭环。” [translation: “Proficient in deep-learning frameworks (primarily PyTorch); able to independently carry an experiment through a closed loop.”] PyTorch is the principal framework, so $R$ and $Q$ are positive while $G$ is not.
- **E0205, PyTorch:** under “硬性要求” [mandatory requirements], “PyTorch 熟练;有仿真(Isaac / MuJoCo)或真机联调经验。” [translation: “Proficient in PyTorch; experience with simulation (Isaac/MuJoCo) or physical-robot integration.”] The choice after the semicolon does not govern PyTorch.
- **N0874, Python:** under *Required Skills*, “Strong Python programming skills.” This gives $R=Q=P$ and $G=Z$.
- **N0878, Python and PyTorch:** under *Required Skills*, “Strong Python skills and deep experience with PyTorch”. The conjunctive statement contributes two technology pairs in one advertisement. The required heading does not also make either pair a $G$ positive.

The three primary clarifications concern explicit example cues. A032 and A099 contain “熟悉PyTorch/TensorFlow等深度学习框架” [translation: “Familiar with deep-learning frameworks such as PyTorch/TensorFlow.”]. A065 contains “有使用PyTorch/TensorFlow、Isaac Gym/Mujoco等工具进行机器人控制相关算法开发的经验。” [translation: “Experience using tools such as PyTorch/TensorFlow and Isaac Gym/MuJoCo to develop robot-control algorithms.”]. In each complete source, “等” governs an illustrative class and no separate unconditional PyTorch expression appears. The revision therefore resolves direct-expression status from $U$ to $Z$ without removing the mention.

A contrasting disputed source is N0149: “熟练使用PyTorch/TensorFlow” [translation: “Proficient in using PyTorch/TensorFlow.”]. The slash has no explicit conjunction, choice or example marker. The primary layer retains unresolved scope; the alternative nonpositive reading is one of the admissible switches. This distinction explains why explicit examples are corrected while bare slash constructions remain a sensitivity question.

## Firm cells pair keys and offline navigation

## Engineering activity decomposition as background

The earlier Galbot–Figure engineering-activity decomposition remains a supplementary composition example. Its fixed four-function comparison contains 82 Galbot and 60 Figure advertisements. Galbot's pooled engineering-activity rate exceeds Figure's by 11.10 percentage points, decomposed arithmetically as $+19.45-8.35$: a positive function-composition component and a negative within-function component. The identity describes these advertisements and does not estimate a firm effect.

The local source audit and the whole-board R1/R2/R3 screens answer different questions. All 142 records in the four-function comparison have captured text and evaluable activity status, establishing denominator recoverability rather than semantic accuracy. R1, R2 and R3 instead retain 17 firms/968 advertisements, 11/576 and 11/557 after successively applying whole-board activity evaluability, source-quality and description-length rules. Galbot fails those broader board screens even though its 82 records in the local example are evaluable.
