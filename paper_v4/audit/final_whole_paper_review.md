# Final whole-paper research review

Date: 26 September 2026. This is a bounded synthesis of the completed research upgrade, source challenges and numerical checks. It is not an editor's decision, an independent human coding study or an exhaustive referee report. The initial quick-review files under `review/supporting/` describe intake of v3 and remain historical.

## Contribution after the upgrades

The defensible contribution is an empirical comparison of explicitly defined vacancy-text constructs. It demonstrates that technology ordering can change within the same advertisements when the qualification relation being counted changes. The paper now distinguishes mention M, GENERAL/REQUIRED context H, unconditional target expression Q, and declared expansions. Existing compositional extraction and contextual technology measurement research is acknowledged; the study does not claim to invent relation-aware extraction.

The strongest case is Python versus PyTorch in 105 robot-learning advertisements. Q favors Python even under adverse assignments of recorded unknowns, whereas admitting the full example-compatible relation set favors PyTorch. Common-mention analysis, exact wording weights, source-level increment inspection and the same-class framework comparison make the result more informative than a juxtaposition of raw counts. Each also limits its interpretation.

The firm-ranking result has been demoted appropriately. A strict eligibility threshold provides just one employer pair; many supported lower-threshold directions have small hypothetical editing distances. Neither thousands of arithmetic checks nor alternative weights make this a representative firm ranking.

## Journal-facing assessment

The revised question is relevant to labor-demand measurement and recruitment information, rather than merely an embodied-AI company inventory. The paper is quantitative and has a reproducible empirical argument. The final Word files and structured abstract are checked against the retrieved IJM format requirements. These facts support preparing the work for an IJM editorial assessment; they do not establish that the journal will find the contribution sufficiently broad or externally validated.

The principal scientific vulnerability is the lack of independent human semantic validation and any behavioral outcome. A reviewer can reasonably ask whether recruiters or applicants interpret the selected constructs similarly. The manuscript makes that limitation visible, reduces its substantive claims accordingly, and does not manufacture a validation statistic.

## Why the present upgrade stops here

Five substantive stages have changed both the evidence and the manuscript. A further regression on the same small purposive frame, more repetitions of the same construct contrast, or a convenience expansion without a new validation design would add volume without resolving the main uncertainty. No remaining detected source, arithmetic or conceptual defect is intentionally left unfixed.

The next high-value evidence would be an independently human-coded validation sample, interviews with requisition owners, an applicant wording experiment or linked screening outcomes. Those are new empirical studies involving evidence and participants not currently available in this workspace. They cannot be replaced by another model opinion or simulated observations represented as real. The current research upgrade therefore closes after reproducibility and document acceptance, without claiming that the topic can never improve.

The user has expressly retained later handling of publisher AI policy and author-controlled submission decisions. This review records that division of work without reopening it as a blocker. No manuscript has been submitted and no private source corpus has been published.
