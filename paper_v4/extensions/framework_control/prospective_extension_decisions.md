## Frozen semantics

M records target visibility within the retained captured fields. H records at least one target occurrence in GENERAL or REQUIRED candidate-qualification context, excluding PREFERRED-only and duty-only occurrences.

Narrow Q retains P/Z/U: P when a known GENERAL or REQUIRED occurrence specifies TensorFlow without an outer choice, example, conditional, or unresolved operator; Z when no positive exists and the available occurrences are excluded under the construct; U when a potentially qualifying occurrence remains unresolved and there is no other known positive. P overrides U when an independent positive exists.

An explicit outer choice or example governs an inner slash. A bare slash with no outer resolution remains U. A bare parenthetical list without a clear example/choice cue remains U or is kept as a separate recorded reading, rather than silently treated as a settled example. Do not choose the reading after inspecting its effect on technology ordering. Preferred-only context remains outside H/Q and the expansions.

Q+E unions current Q positives with known EXAMPLE qualification occurrences. Q+C+E additionally admits known CHOICE/CHOICE_AND_EXAMPLE. Chinese 等-only open lists are recorded separately from explicit such as/e.g./如 and from bare parentheses. Existing PyTorch classifications are not altered if the new source review suggests a difference: record and report every same-clause/operator inconsistency.
