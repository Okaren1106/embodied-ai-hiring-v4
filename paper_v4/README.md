# V4.0: Technology qualifications in embodied AI hiring

The pre-application-form research paper is **What Counts as a Technology Qualification in Embodied AI Hiring?** It compares technology mention, qualification context and unconditional target-specific expression in 248 advertisements, and tests how examples and choices change technology ordering.
